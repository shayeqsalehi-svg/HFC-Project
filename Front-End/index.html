COMPANY OVERVIEW

Established in 2020 in Kabul, Afghanistan, Hewad Future Consultancy (HFC) is a multidisciplinary professional consulting and advisory firm. We provide integrated advisory, consulting, technology, management, financial, risk, organizational development, and capacity-building services to public institutions, private companies, financial institutions, NGOs, development organizations, and entrepreneurs..

Strategic Positioning

Strategic Positioning

Hewad Future Consultancy positions itself as an integrated, multidisciplinary advisory firm connecting eight core organizational dimensions:

  • Strategy & Management | Finance & Risk Management
  • Governance & Compliance | Technology & Digital Transformation
  • People & Human Capital | Operations & Process Excellence

This integrated approach enables organizations to address complex challenges from multiple dimensions rather than treating individual problems in isolation. By bringing together diverse areas of expertise, HFC helps clients align strategy, people, processes, technology, financial performance, risk, and governance to deliver solutions that are technically sound, practical, implementable, cost-effective, and sustainable.
